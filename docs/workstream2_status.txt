NACC74 MIXED-PROTOCOL MRI PREFLIGHT
===================================
Status: PRIMARY_TRAINING_COMPLETE; SIX EXPLORATORY COMPARISONS COMPLETE.
Updated: 2026-10-28

This audit supersedes workstream2_clariti_legacy.txt. The approved experiment
uses NACC Freeze 74 UDS clinical history and NACC Mixed-protocol quantitative
MRI only. No CLARiTI or PET data were used in this preflight.

MRI preparation audit
---------------------
The MRI export contains 12,180 scan rows. The preflight found 519 rows that
were exact duplicates by composite site/participant key and scan date, with
identical availability and all 152 candidate measurements. These exact copies
are collapsed in memory; conflicting same-date rows block the run.
The RDD-reviewed candidate whitelist contains 152 variables. The 20% missing
filter uses only 69 quantitative MRI visits in the training split as its
denominator. 149 features are retained after missingness and non-constant
checks. Only training MRI visits fit the imputer and scaler.
Historical visits without MRI are preserved as clinical visits and represented
by a normalized zero MRI vector plus an all-missing MRI mask. Every anchor has
quantitative MRI. Train/validation/test histories are disjoint by participant.

Primary model result
--------------------
The multimodal longitudinal GRU selected its checkpoint at epoch 2 (12 epochs
were run with early stopping). On the 12-person test set (3 progressions),
AUROC was 0.630, AUPRC 0.360, balanced accuracy 0.500, sensitivity 0.000,
specificity 1.000, F1 0.000, and Brier score 0.233. It detected 0/3
progressions at the validation-selected threshold. The 95% patient-bootstrap
AUROC interval was approximately 0.30-0.91.

Limitations and remaining actions
---------------------------------
The sample is small: only 12 test participants, including 3 progressions.
All test metrics and confidence intervals are highly uncertain and must not
support clinical utility or calibration claims. The primary model has poor
sensitivity (0/3 progressions detected). Since the test has been inspected, a
confirmatory model comparison requires a new untouched independent test set.
The training/checkpoint/metrics/learning-curve/XAI artifacts exist privately;
no narrative report was generated.
Do not use the test set for model selection. Do not commit any participant-level
source/derived data, pseudonymization key, checkpoint, or preprocessing object.
